XFORCE

A single-player tactical FPS prototype. Deploy into a wind-scoured training compound, clear hostile waves, and keep moving. Built as a lightweight browser game; no external art assets required.

CONTROLS
WASD move  ·  Mouse aim  ·  LMB fire  ·  RMB aim  ·  Shift sprint
R reload  ·  C crouch  ·  Esc pause/release mouse
Single-player AI is playable now. The combat loop is organized for future server-authoritative multiplayer; network play is not included in this prototype.
